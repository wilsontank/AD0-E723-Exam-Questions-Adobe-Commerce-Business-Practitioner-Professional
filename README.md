## What Is the AD0-E723 Adobe Commerce Business Practitioner Professional Exam?

The Adobe Commerce Business Practitioner Professional certification is designed for professionals who work with Adobe Commerce from a business, operational, or solution perspective. Adobe lists the certification at the Professional level and recommends 0–12 months of experience. 

The exam focuses on whether candidates can apply Adobe Commerce concepts in realistic business situations, such as choosing the correct product type, configuring promotions, understanding order status, managing customer groups, structuring websites and stores, and handling privacy and compliance requirements. 

Typical job roles include:

- Product Owners
- Consultants
- Adobe Commerce Strategists
- eCommerce Managers and Administrators

## Recommended Experience Before Taking AD0-E723

Adobe recommends 0–12 months of hands-on experience with Magento Open Source using the LUMA or BLANK theme, together with a basic understanding of Adobe Commerce. 

Candidates should also be familiar with:
- Digital marketing concepts such as SEO, CMS, email marketing, cross-sell, upsell, analytics, reporting, and monitoring
- eCommerce terminology, including omnichannel and multichannel strategies
- Pricing and promotions
- Customer purchase journeys
- Order lifecycle
- Magento Marketplace and third-party extensions
- Security and compliance concepts such as GDPR, ADA, PCI DSS, and PSD2

## 1. Catalog and Products — 22%

This is the largest exam section and focuses on how products are configured, priced, displayed, and managed in Adobe Commerce.

- Given a scenario, identify which product type should be used.
- Given a configuration, identify how the product will behave on the storefront.
- Given a scenario, identify which pricing configuration to use.
- Given a scenario, configure appropriate product attributes and catalog navigation.
- Explain how to manage product inventory for single and multi-location setups.

## 2. Promotions — 16%

This domain focuses on pricing and promotional rules.

- Given a scenario, configure the appropriate promotion type.
- Given a promotion setup, explain the final price.
- Given a scenario, troubleshoot promotion issues.

## 3. Orders, Payments, Shipping, and Tax — 18%

This section measures knowledge of the order lifecycle and the commercial processes that occur after a customer begins checkout.

- Given a scenario, interpret order state/status to decide allowed actions.
- Given a scenario, differentiate between online and offline payment methods.
- Given business requirements, configure appropriate shipping and tax behavior.

## 4. Customers — 14%

This domain focuses on customer accounts, targeting, segmentation, and privacy.

- Explain how to use customer groups and segments to target pricing and content.
- Given a scenario, appropriately configure customer accounts and attributes.
- Given a scenario, explain how to assist a shopper while respecting privacy.

## 5. Adobe Commerce Features and Services — 18%

This section covers broader platform capabilities and administrative functions.

- Given a scenario, determine the appropriate website/store/store-view structure.
- Explain how to configure admin roles and permissions in the admin panel.
- Differentiate between Magento Open Source, Adobe Commerce, and Adobe Commerce Cloud.
- Given a scenario, determine appropriate CMS and personalization features.
- Identify available reports, import/export capabilities, and Commerce services.

## 6. eCommerce General Knowledge — 12%

This domain focuses on broader eCommerce principles that apply to Adobe Commerce operations.

- Identify key data privacy, accessibility, and compliance considerations.
- Given a scenario, determine whether a payment flow is PCI-compliant.
- Identify basic SEO-relevant features in Adobe Commerce.

## How to Prepare for the AD0-E723 Exam

### 1. Learn Adobe Commerce Through Business Scenarios

Do not study features in isolation. Practice asking which configuration best fits a particular product, promotion, customer, checkout, or storefront requirement.

### 2. Focus Heavily on Catalog and Product Configuration

Since Catalog and Products is the largest domain, spend extra time on product types, product attributes, pricing, navigation, and inventory behavior.

### 3. Understand the Full Order and Promotion Lifecycle

Learn how promotions affect price and how order state, payment, shipping, and tax decisions influence what happens after checkout.

### 4. Review Platform Structure and Customer Management

Make sure you understand website/store/store-view architecture, admin roles, customer groups, segments, accounts, and personalization features.
